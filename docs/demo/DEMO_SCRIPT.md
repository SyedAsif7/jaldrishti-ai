## Detailed Step-by-Step Script

### Minute 0:00 – 0:25 | Step 1: Marathwada Drought Overview
- **Screen:** Home Screen (`Overview Dashboard`) with all 8 districts displayed.
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"Respected jury members, welcome to JalDrishti AI. On your screen is the synoptic water intelligence overview of Maharashtra's Marathwada division. Marathwada is chronically vulnerable to structural drought, with rainfall, groundwater, and dam storage historically trapped in disconnected government silos. JalDrishti AI unifies these streams into a single operational canvas. Notice our regional composite risk score of 61.6 out of 100—highlighting that five out of eight districts are currently under high or severe drought vulnerability."*

---

### Minute 0:25 – 0:45 | Step 2: District Selection – Parbhani
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Drought Risk Map** (`/map`) and click on **Parbhani** (or select Parbhani from top selector).
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"Let us focus on our proposed pilot district—Parbhani. Clicking Parbhani instantly synchronizes all GIS polygon layers, indicators, and charts. Parbhani exhibits an elevated composite risk score of 59.9, placing it in the High Risk tier. Rather than waiting for retrospective post-monsoon audits in late autumn, the system immediately flags compounding stress across its nine talukas."*

---

### Minute 0:45 – 1:10 | Step 3: Rainfall Deficit & Historical Comparison
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Rainfall Intelligence** (`/rainfall`).
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"Here, the platform evaluates precipitation against the 30-year IMD Long Period Average. Parbhani received 512.6 mm against a normal of 774.0 mm—a departure of -33.8%, classified as Deficient. Below, our statistical engine fits historical precipitation to a World Meteorological Organization Standardized Precipitation Index. SPI-3 stands at -1.18, confirming moderate agricultural drought, exacerbated by a 21-day dry spell during critical kharif crop flowering."*

---

### Minute 1:10 – 1:35 | Step 4: Groundwater Stress Indicators
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Groundwater Monitoring** (`/groundwater`).
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"Groundwater is Marathwada's lifeline. In Parbhani, post-monsoon depth to the water table has dropped to 9.2 meters below ground level, plunging past 13.4 meters in pre-monsoon observations. Over 68% of observation wells show water table exhaustion. Our model highlights Pathri and Manwath talukas as critically over-extracted with extraction ratios exceeding 84%, warning that natural recharge will be insufficient for winter crops."*

---

### Minute 1:35 – 2:00 | Step 5: Reservoir Water-Storage Information
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Reservoir & Storage** (`/reservoirs`).
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"Surface water reserves are tracked daily from Water Resources Department bulletins. Parbhani’s primary irrigation source, the Lower Dudhana Project in Selu, holds only 36.8% live storage against a gross capacity of 7,950 Mcft. Combined with medium dams, aggregate district storage is at 38.0%, providing an estimated 145 drinking water security days. Preemptive canal budgeting prevents premature dead-storage exhaustion."*

---

### Minute 2:00 – 2:25 | Step 6: Explainable Drought-Risk Assessment (MCDA)
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Drought Risk Analysis** (`/analysis`).
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"This is the core innovation of JalDrishti AI: explainable, deterministic Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis. We eliminate black-box opacity. Officials see exactly how the 59.9 score is formed: 35% rainfall deficit, 30% groundwater drop, 25% reservoir storage, and 10% soil moisture. Every weight is auditable, allowing evaluators to adjust sensitivity sliders in real time to model policy contingencies."*

---

### Minute 2:25 – 2:45 | Step 7: Recommended Water-Management Actions
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Alerts & Advisories** (`/alerts`).
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"JalDrishti AI translates complex analytics into departmental action: advising the Agriculture Department to enforce drip irrigation schedules in Selu and Pathri, guiding the District Disaster Management Authority to ring-fence Lower Dudhana drinking quotas, and prioritizing 42 percolation tanks along Dudhna sub-basins for desilting under Jalyukt Shivar Abhiyan."*

---

### Minute 2:45 – 3:00 | Step 8: District Report Generation & Conclusion
- **Screen:** Navigate to **Reports & Sources** (`/reports`), click **Download Parbhani CSV**, and hover over **Print Dossier**.
- **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"Finally, district collectors can export complete tabular CSV dossiers or generate print-ready executive water security briefs with a single click. JalDrishti AI has 41 passing automated tests, is fully open-source, and is ready for a 90-day pilot in Parbhani. Thank you."*
