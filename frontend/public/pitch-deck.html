Slide 01 • Problem Statement & Need

The Silent Crisis: Fragmented Water Information in Marathwada

Recurring structural drought meets administrative data silos, turning predictable water stress into costly emergency relief.

18.7M Citizens
8 Districts • 76 Talukas

⚠️ Chronic Regional Vulnerability

  • • Rain-Shadow Hard Rock: Western Ghats rain shadow with only 650–880 mm erratic monsoon rainfall; Deccan basalt aquifers lack storage depth.
  • • Rapid Well Depletion: >70% of GSDA monitoring wells register >2m seasonal water table drops below decadal averages.
  • • Surface Water Exhaustion: Division reservoirs (Jayakwadi, Yeldari) regularly hit dead-storage levels by April.

🏛️ The Departmental Data Silo Bottleneck

IMD / Mahavedh → Rainfall Deficits Isolated
GSDA → Observation Wells (m bgl) Quarterly Silo
Irrigation (WRD) → Dam Live Storage % Separate Portal

Result: District Collectors only act in late autumn—after Kharif crops wilt and emergency tankers cost hundreds of crores.
